A weekend trip into the Otways led by two SGEAS staff. Saturday focuses on the Otway Group sediments and the Twelve Apostles coastline; Sunday covers Cretaceous fossil sites and palaeoenvironmental reconstruction.

Cost covers transport and one night of shared accommodation. Bring sturdy boots, sun protection and a hand lens if you have one.
